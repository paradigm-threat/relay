# Competition: free problem-solving AI + image scan + local handoff

**Product we are comparing against**

A free, no-card, problem-oriented AI that:

- Accepts continuous / generous image scanning (glasses, plumbing, health triage, mushrooms, etc.)
- Talks / voices through a case until the person is done or stuck
- States legal stops every time
- Hands a full investigation dossier (photos + AI notes) to a local provider who can bid or decline before any meeting
- Is **not** a general “everything LLM”
- Monetizes later via settled provider connections, not consumer subscriptions

**Last researched:** 2026-11-07 (US stack) · **2026-11-07 Israel refresh** (Hebrew AI-pro apps + LLM adoption)  
**Status:** No product found that matches this exact stack end-to-end (anon tickets, deferred Projects, place-tied KB, businesses $0 forever, Relay tip). Israel has **more** AI→local-pro apps than Georgia.

---

## Verdict (one screen)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Exact same service, free, no card? | **No.** |
| Free unlimited general chat AI? | **Yes** (ChatGPT Free text; Gemini Free text — tool quotas still apply). |
| Free generous / “unlimited” consumer image *search*? | **Closest: Google Lens** (visual search, not a case file + local bid). |
| Free real-time problem-solving camera loop for trades + health + nature + dossier handoff? | **Not found.** |
| Closest paid / freemium stack? | **US:** Thumbtack AI, Angi, Fixt/Fixxy. **Israel:** Hendiman, DontSearch, AI Trust, Spetz/Shpitz-class. |
| Free vertical specialists? | **Seek / iNaturalist** (nature), **Ada** (symptom triage), **Lens** (lookup). Each is narrow. |
| Israel vs Georgia competition density? | **Israel denser** on Hebrew AI→handyman apps; **Georgia** competes more with Thumbtack/Angi + DIY LLM. |

Our wedge is not “another chat model.” It is **service-oriented investigation**: capture → queue → batch Projects, legal stops, dossier, local bid, place-tied KB — free for the consumer until habit forms, no card wall, businesses $0 forever.

---

## Exact match?

**Nothing public today sells the full product as one free app:**

1. Unlimited (or effectively unlimited for problem-solving) image + voice investigation  
2. No trial / no card up front  
3. Structured case file a stranger can open before agreeing to work  
4. Local bid / decline with AI assessment already done  
5. Scope limited to solving real-world service problems (not creative writing, coding, general chat)

Pieces exist. The combination does not.

## Tier A — Closest competitors (AI + problem + local help)

### Thumbtack (AI-powered home experience)

- **What it does:** Homeowner describes the issue with **text, photos, or voice**; AI scopes the project and matches local pros; project hub for the job. Nationwide testing with broader 2026 rollout.  
- **Free for homeowner?** Posting / matching is the consumer path; Thumbtack monetizes **pros** (intro / lead economics), not a ChatGPT-style subscription.  
- **Same as us?** Closest **marketplace** competitor for home repair. Optimizes for **hiring a pro**, not for finishing half the jobs with free AI alone. No evidence of unlimited real-time scanning as the primary free product, nor cross-domain (health, mushrooms, glasses) investigation.  
- **Card up front?** Not required for the typical homeowner browse/match flow (pro pays for leads).  
- **Threat level:** **High** in home services only. They have supply (pros) and brand. We win only if solo AI resolution + dossier quality + non-home verticals + no-salary / Relay distribution are clearly better.

### Angi (AI Helper + ChatGPT surface)

- **What it does:** AI turns homeowner description into service requests; ChatGPT integration for guidance + localized pro recommendations (2026 press).  
- **Same as us?** Same family as Thumbtack: **lead marketplace with AI intake**, not free unlimited investigation as the product.  
- **Threat level:** **High** in home services; same caveats.

### Fixt / Fixxy-class products

- **What they claim:** AI photo diagnosis, bidding, sometimes checkout; Fixxy consumer app markets “snap a pic” diagnosis + local pros; provider side often **trial + card** then subscription.  
- **Same as us?** Closer to “photo → scope → bid” than Lens. Consumer freeness and “no card” vary; provider tools are **not** free unlimited AI for everyone.  
- **Threat level:** **Medium** — product shape overlaps; scale and free depth unclear.

### TaskRabbit / Nextdoor / Craigslist

- **What they do:** Local help / gigs / posts. Little or no serious free AI image investigation.  
- **Threat level:** **Low** as AI products; **Medium** as “find a person” substitutes when users skip investigation entirely.

Hebrew-first apps that combine **AI intake** with **local בעלי מקצוע**. Closer to the *marketplace half* of MyProblem than most Georgia-local products. Still **hire-first**, not deferred thorough Projects + place-tied KB + Relay tip + businesses free forever.

## Tier B — Free AI chat / vision (no card), but not our product

### ChatGPT Free (OpenAI)

- **No card** for Free tier.  
- **Text:** Everyday chats effectively unlimited (abuse safeguards).  
- **Images / files:** Free capped at about **3 file uploads per day** (official upload help); images up to 20 MB. Voice and other tools have separate limits.  
- **Fits us?** Strong free *advisor* for a few photos/day. **Fails** batch Projects, case dossier for providers, place-tied KB, and local bid. General LLM, not service-ops UI.  
- **Threat level:** **High for “just ask the AI” substitutes**; **Low for our full loop.**  
- **IL + US:** Same product; ChatGPT remains the default DIY assistant in both markets (IL surveys: ~75% of AI users cite ChatGPT).

### Google Gemini Free

- **No card** for free assistant access.  
- Image / live camera conversation available; **quotas change** by model and demand (not a published “unlimited scan” product).  
- **Fits us?** Same pattern as ChatGPT: free help with limits; no provider dossier marketplace.  
- **Threat level:** Same as ChatGPT.  
- **IL:** Fast-rising second tool (~42% of AI users in Bezeq-class 2025 reporting; strong with teens).

### Grok (xAI)

- Consumer free/paid tiers via X; smaller share than ChatGPT/Gemini in US and IL surveys.  
- **Fits us?** Another general assistant substitute — not a local service ticket product.  
- **Threat level:** Low–medium as DIY chat; **not** a local-pro network.

### Claude / other free consumer LLMs

- Free tiers exist with **rate limits**; not positioned as unlimited field scanning + local trades.  
- **Threat level:** Low–medium substitute for text advice.  
- **IL note:** Anthropic geographic data has shown **very high Claude use per capita** in Israel (small tech-forward market); still not a Midrag/Hendiman substitute.

### Microsoft Copilot / Bing Visual Search

- Free visual search and chat with Microsoft account; search-oriented, not case + bid.  
- **Threat level:** Low–medium for identification; low for marketplace.

## Tier C — Free image identification (narrow or search-shaped)

### Google Lens

- **Free, no paid consumer tier**, no card. Visual search: objects, text, plants, shopping, similar images. Available in Google app / Camera / Chrome.  
- **Unlimited?** Consumer Lens is widely treated as **uncapped for normal use** (developer Cloud Vision is paid separately).  
- **Fits us?** Best **free image lookup** in the world. Returns **search results**, not a service case file, legal-stop workflow, or local bidder with your photos packaged. Not real-time “investigate until solved” UX for trades.  
- **Threat level:** **High** as the default “point camera” habit; **Low** as a full substitute for dossier + bid.

### Apple Visual Intelligence / Visual Look Up

- Built into supported iPhones; free with device. Identify plants, animals, etc.  
- **Fits us?** Device-native lookup; no cross-platform case file or provider network.  
- **Threat level:** Medium on iPhone for ID; low for marketplace.

### Seek by iNaturalist

- **Free**, often no account required for core ID; strong for **plants / wildlife / fungi**.  
- **Fits us?** Excellent free vertical for mushrooms/nature. No plumbing/health/trades, no local service bid.  
- **Threat level:** High in nature ID only.

### Specialty ID apps (Picture Insect, Eyedentify, plant IDs, etc.)

- Mostly **freemium**: e.g. Eyedentify ~**2 IDs/day** free, then subscription; many plant/insect apps wall after a few scans.  
- **Fits us?** Prove demand for photo ID; **opposite** of “unlimited free problem-solving.”  
- **Threat level:** Low on pricing model; they train users that scans are scarce.

## Tier D — Health and human experts

### Ada Health (symptom checker)

- **Free** consumer symptom assessment; triage-oriented; monetizes B2B.  
- **Fits us?** Closest free **health problem** flow. Not general image scanning for trades; not a local provider dossier marketplace (though it routes care intensity).  
- **Threat level:** High in health triage UX/trust; we must not claim to beat Ada on clinical rigor day one — we partner/stop/redirect.

### JustAnswer

- Photo to a human expert possible; **not free** (membership / category pricing; no “unlimited free AI”).  
- **Fits us?** Human escalation pattern we might eventually mirror with per-case licensed pay — but they charge the consumer up front.  
- **Threat level:** Medium for people willing to pay for a human now.

### Teladoc / local telehealth / urgent care apps

- Card + visit fees; regulated.  
- **Fits us?** Destination after our legal stop — not free AI competitors.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| General chat AI | Yes (ChatGPT / Gemini Free) | Text yes; **tools capped** | No | No |
| Image understanding in chat | Yes, with caps | ChatGPT Free ~**3 uploads/day** | Ad hoc chat only | No |
| Visual search | Yes (Lens) | Effectively generous | No | No |
| Nature ID | Yes (Seek) | Generous | Narrow | No |
| Symptom triage | Yes (Ada) | Free consumer | Health only | Care routing, not trade bids |
| Home photo → pro (US) | Thumbtack / Angi (homeowner) | Not “unlimited AI product” | Partial project scope | **Yes** (marketplace) |
| Home photo → pro (IL) | Hendiman / DontSearch / AI Trust | Free consumer paths; pro sub or paid tiers | Partial diagnose + match | **Yes** (marketplace) |
| **Our full stack** | — | Planned | Planned | Planned |

**Bottom line on “is there full image scanning for free?”**  
- **Lookup / lookup:** yes — **Google Lens**.  
- **Conversational diagnosis from photos:** yes but **metered** on major free LLMs.  
- **Unlimited real-time service investigation + dossier + bid, no card:** **not found.**

**Bottom line on “is there free AI?”**  
- Yes, for chat and limited vision.  
- Not yes for **unlimited problem-ops AI** positioned as infrastructure for services.

## What would offer “the same thing” without trial or card?

Realistically, only these come close on *price wall*:

1. **Google Lens + ChatGPT/Gemini Free** — user stitches the workflow themselves (no dossier, no bid, hit upload caps).  
2. **Thumbtack / Angi** — free for the homeowner to describe/photo and get pros; **not** free unlimited AI as the product, and not multi-domain investigation.  
3. **Ada + Seek + Lens** — free vertical tools the user must already know to open.

No incumbent markets: “use unlimited problem-solving AI with live scanning, no card, then optionally hand the whole investigation to a local plumber/optician/caretaker who already sees the answer attempt.”

## Live / realtime camera analysis (who has what)

Our planned shape: **live preview on device → clarity/uniqueness gate → sparse uploads → rate-limited server → Problem patch** (not continuous 30 fps to a frontier model).

| Who | Live camera AI? | Selective frame gate + server rate limit (public)? | Problem ticket + local bid dossier? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Gemini Live / Search Live / Lens Live** (Project Astra) | **Yes** — continuous camera (and screen) conversation | Undocumented consumer gate; feels like stream-to-model | No |
| **ChatGPT Voice + live video** | **Yes** — paid tiers (Plus/Pro/Biz/Edu) | Undocumented; chat-shaped | No |
| **Apple Visual Intelligence** | Camera mode; often **locks a still** for ID/actions | On-device search path; not our sparse-upload architecture | No |
| **Microsoft Copilot Vision** | Screen/camera-style live help (assistant) | Assistant, not service Problem + bid | No |
| **Google Lens** (classic) | Live viewfinder search | Search results, not investigation ticket | No |
| **Thumbtack / Angi / Fixxy** | Photo / voice / text intake; video = call pro, not live AI stream diagnosis | Snap/upload, not gated realtime frame factory | Partial project→pro; not our loop |
| **LightlyEdge** (SDK) | Edge frame selection for fleets/robots | **Yes** — keep novel/high-value frames (B2B data collection) | No consumer service product |
| **Framesieve / Argus / Savant-class** | Open-source or toolkit realtime video AI | Pieces of gate / on-device CV | Not a consumer marketplace |
| **Us (planned)** | Yes | Explicit product feature | Yes |

**Closest UX for “point camera and talk”:** Gemini Live and ChatGPT live video.  
**Closest engineering for “don’t upload every frame”:** industrial SDKs (LightlyEdge) and research/self-host stacks — not consumer service apps.  
**Nobody found** combining gated realtime frames + Problem tickets + provider dossier bid + free/Spot vs paid reliable pools.

## Competitive risks to watch

1. **Thumbtack/Angi** (US) and **Hendiman / DontSearch / AI Trust** (IL) ship “good enough” photo/voice→pro and own or rent pro graphs.  
2. **Google** deepens Lens + Gemini into a continuous camera agent (still unlikely to become a local bid network soon).  
3. **OpenAI** raises Free image limits or ships a “camera mode” that eats casual use — same risk in GA and IL.  
4. **Israeli AI-pro startups** race on Hebrew UX + WhatsApp-adjacent habits before we have supply.  
5. **Freemium ID apps** race to the bottom on price but stay subscription-shaped.  
6. **Regulation** in health/fungi advice forces everyone (including us) into stop-and-redirect — advantage to whoever designs stops as product.

## LLM adoption — same stack in GA and Israel?

**Yes — same global assistants; Israel is not “behind.”** If anything, consumer AI familiarity is high in both, and IL per-capita use of some tools (e.g. Claude) has ranked among the world’s highest for a small tech economy.

| Signal | US / Georgia context | Israel |
|---|---|---|
| Primary DIY LLMs | ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot; Grok smaller | ChatGPT dominant; Gemini rising fast; Claude/Grok smaller share |
| Adult LLM / AI use | ~half of US adults use LLMs (2025 surveys) | High awareness/use in Bezeq-class internet reports; Microsoft adoption index ~34% adults (country rank mid-teens) |
| Threat to MyProblem | High for casual advice; low for full ticket/Project/KB loop | **Same**, plus local AI-pro apps already wrapping AI→pro |

Do **not** assume IL users need “education that AI exists.” Assume they already tried ChatGPT/Gemini and maybe Hendiman — pitch **local KB + thorough deferred Projects + free shops**.

## Implications for GA + Israel pilots

- Measure against **Lens + ChatGPT/Gemini Free** as the DIY baseline in **both** regions.  
- **Georgia:** measure against **Thumbtack/Angi** in home verticals.  
- **Israel:** measure against **Hendiman, DontSearch, AI Trust, Midrag** (+ WhatsApp “ask a group”) — not Thumbtack alone.  
- Do **not** claim unique “free AI”; claim unique **free investigation loop** (capture → queue → batch) **+ dossier handoff + place-tied KB + no-card depth + businesses $0**.  
- Health: treat Ada-class triage as partner/redirect, not something to out-diagnose.  
- Nature: Seek is the free bar for mushrooms; our value is legal stop + when to involve a human + dossier if needed.  
- IL product: Hebrew/bilingual strings early; expect denser local AI-pro noise.
