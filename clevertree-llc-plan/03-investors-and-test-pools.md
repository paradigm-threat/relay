# Investors and test pools

## How to approach investors

**Do not** pitch a normal SaaS / headcount company.

**Do** pitch a short, capped growth check into a Georgia LLC that:

- Burns almost everything on free investigation + match infra
- Has **no payroll line**
- Returns capital as **distributions** after automatic per-case splits
- Uses Relay + Cursor-built product, not a hired engineering department funded by the round

### One-page story

**MyProblem (myproblem.app):** local businesses join at **$0** and stay free — unsure customers open **tickets** (site link / app: audio + stills), not a phone we answer; when help is needed shops get a Problem dossier first (and may ignore). The service is free forever. A user should never expect to enter a card. If the help was good, they may leave a gratuity for the person, the AI, or both. $1, $5, and $10 are examples. That gift is the signal a subscription cannot make, and it is how hosting gets kept up. Low income gets the same help. No salaries. **Future VoIP** (automated into the same ticket pipeline) is a planned, worthwhile infra investment — not in the day-one product.

Say explicitly: this is not a hiring plan; we are **not** a lead-gen company that invoices shops. Their money does not buy a team.

**No guaranteed return.** A gratuity to hosting might later cover the AI bill in a busy region. It does not promise that a hardware check comes back. A Relay node can start small. The model bill cannot: a two-region vision pilot is on the order of **$700–$2,000 a month** ([`08-realtime-vision-infra-cost.md`](08-realtime-vision-infra-cost.md)). Covering $500/month from infrastructure gifts alone, at a $5 average, takes about 100 such gifts a month — roughly 1,000 monthly users if 10% tip the machines $5, or about 5,000 if only 2% do. A new region will not have that at the start.

Against a **$1 subscription from every monthly user**, infrastructure gifts tie when (share who tip the machines) × (average gift) = $1. Examples, not measurements: 20% × $5 or 10% × $10 ties; 10% × $5 raises about $0.50; 30% × $5 raises about $1.50. A delighted person can beat $1. The average does not, unless many people do. The hard problem is getting the person who was helped to tip the **machines**, not only the human. We refuse a subscription because it charges people the product did not delight and blocks people who cannot pay — not because tips are safer cash.

### Who to talk to

- Angels who get **free forever + a voluntary gratuity** (not classic per-lead SaaS, and not a subscription)
- People who want **LLC distributions**
- Partners who will run **compatible local hardware** on the **same ticket fabric** (world network) — not forkers who want a private incompatible clone
- Skip most VC until a metro shows free help that people were glad enough to tip, and/or ticket deflection for signed-up shops (web/app intake; VoIP later)

### World network — intent (not a deal yet)

Public site + Terms state **intent only** (no offer / no signed obligations until a separate contract):

- Hardware / region investors must keep stacks **interoperable** with the shared MyProblem / Relay ticket system so Problems stay findable under the same conditions everywhere.
- **No splintering** into walled or incompatible networks — that defeats the vision.
- **Gratuities meant for hosting go mostly to local hardware handlers**; center stays zero-payroll posture.
- Early adopters may later need a written agreement; the website pitch alone does not create one.

Canonical public copy: https://myproblem.app/how-we-run · https://myproblem.app/terms §8 and §11.

### Instrument (simple)

- Cash in
- Preferred return until repaid
- Then fixed profit share
- Avoid a huge preferred stack built for Series A

### Five numbers they will ask (bring ranges)

1. Unit cost of a free real-time scan (backbone + storage + bandwidth)
2. Share of people who were helped and then chose to leave a gratuity, and the amounts they actually leave ($1, $5, $10 are examples, not a price list)
3. Share of Problems that resolve without a business vs hand off with dossier
4. How gifts split in practice — person, AI/hosting, or both — **not** a shop invoice and **not** a subscription
5. Months to return the check at zero salary — **and** the same math if founders later take a draw  
   *(Plan B only: what a shop fee would do to growth — we prefer never)*

### What not to lead with

- Health marketplace / doctors on day one
- “We will raise to hire”
- Exact same claim as “free ChatGPT” (free chat AI already exists; our claim is the investigation loop + dossier)

### Raise sequence (preferred)

1. Form Georgia LLC  
2. Cheap GA + Israel pilot on Relay + backbone APIs, almost no ads  
3. Raise aggressive free campaign against measured cost-per-useful-case and cost-per-settled-match  

Raising first for maximum spread is possible only if labeled clearly as a **growth burn**. There is no required fee to cover it. Gifts arrive only when the help was actually good.

## Test pools: Georgia and Israel

| Pool | Why | What to prove |
|---|---|---|
| **Georgia** | Home of the LLC, English product, US rails, trades you can recruit | Free scans completed; % AI-only close; % dossier; providers who open file; bids; settled jobs. Compete vs Thumbtack/Angi + ChatGPT/Gemini DIY ([`01-competition.md`](01-competition.md)). |
| **Israel** | Second culture/language; brother as local operator; **denser AI→pro app scene** (Hendiman, DontSearch, AI Trust, Midrag) | Hebrew/simple UI trust; local licensing norms; same metrics + win on deferred Projects / local KB vs hire-first AI apps. Same global LLM substitutes as US. |

### Brother’s role (Israel)

Not a salaried employee. On-the-ground cut of settled cases **or** fixed bounty per activated provider, paid from pilot budget / settlements. Keeps the no-salary machine intact.

### Pilot constraints

- One or two verticals only (e.g. plumbing + simple glasses)
- Photo + voice + big buttons
- No doctor marketplace in pilot
- Money rails: US LLC + clear disclosure; do not invent a second entity until volume forces it

### Success definition

Not download count.

1. “Free scan was useful” (solo close or clear next step)  
2. “Provider bid from the dossier without a meeting”

### DIY baseline to beat

Users can already stitch **Google Lens + ChatGPT/Gemini Free**. Pilot must beat that on depth, continuity of scanning, legal stops, and provider-ready dossier — especially where free LLM image uploads are capped (~3/day on ChatGPT Free as of research date).
