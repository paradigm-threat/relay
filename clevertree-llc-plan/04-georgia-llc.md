# Georgia LLC formation

**Not legal advice.** Confirm with Georgia Corporations Division and a Georgia attorney/CPA before filing.

## Why Georgia, why new

- Operating / living base for the restart  
- Arizona Clevertree Solutions, LLC was **terminated** (voluntary termination does not reinstate like some admin dissolutions)  
- New state = new entity, new EIN, new bank account, new merchant account  

## Name

1. Preferred public brand: **MyProblem** with domain **myproblem.app** (registered; live on Vercel).  
2. Search [Georgia Corporations Division](https://ecorp.sos.ga.gov) for **MyProblem**, My Problem, MyProblem Solutions, variants.  
3. Arizona Clevertree history does **not** reserve any name in Georgia and is not the consumer brand.  
4. If exact LLC name is taken, file a distinguishable legal name and DBA **MyProblem**.  
5. Optional defensive: other TLDs later; **myproblem.app** is the public face.

## Formation checklist (typical domestic LLC)

| Step | Notes |
|---|---|
| Choose name | Must be distinguishable; include LLC designator as required |
| Registered agent | Georgia street address; can be yourself if resident, or a service |
| Articles of Organization | File with Georgia Secretary of State (online eCorp typical) |
| Filing fee | Confirm current fee on SOS site at filing time |
| Operating agreement | **Critical for this model** — no salaries; distribution waterfall; investor preferred return; per-case operator splits; what “compensation” means |
| EIN | IRS online application for the new LLC |
| Georgia tax accounts | As applicable (withholding usually N/A if no employees; sales/use if selling taxable services — confirm with CPA) |
| Business bank account | EIN + formation docs; keep personal and Relay mesh money separate |
| Merchant / processor | Stripe or similar in LLC name; expect reserves on new marketplace-like flows |
| Licenses | City/county business license where required; professional licensing is on **providers**, not usually on a lead/dossier platform — still verify for health-adjacent claims |
| Insurance | General liability early; professional / E&O before health-adjacent marketing; cyber if holding case images |

## Operating agreement must say (draft themes)

- Purpose: investigation platform + match; not a clinical practice  
- No employee salaries unless members later amend (default: transaction-embedded pay only)  
- Member distributions after preferred return and reserves  
- Related-party use of Relay infra (license / cost allocation so investors know what they do and do not own)  
- IP assignment: app and brand in the LLC; Relay mesh stays with its owners unless explicitly sold  
- Dissolution and Israel pilot contractor / bounty rules

## What investors own

Unless the deal says otherwise: membership interest in the **Georgia LLC** (product, brand, processor relationships, case-product IP you assign in). They do **not** automatically own the Relay gateway fleet or other Relay open-source/mesh projects.

## Israel note

Pilot users and providers in Israel do not require an Israeli company on day one if the contracting party is the Georgia LLC and disclosures are clear. Local tax/VAT and data rules may still apply — get a short consult before Hebrew marketing spend.
